Common questions

Where is my family's data kept?

On your iPhone, and — if you are signed in to iCloud — in your own private iCloud database, so your records survive a lost phone and stay in step across your devices. There is no SwapSafe account and no SwapSafe server. We cannot see your data. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

Does scanning a barcode send anything about us?

No. The app sends only the barcode number to the Open Food Facts database to look up that product's name and ingredients. Nothing about you or your children is included.

I deleted the app and my records came back. Why?

Deleting the app removes the copy on that device but not the copy in your iCloud account, so reinstalling restores it. To remove the iCloud copy too, go to Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage, choose SwapSafe and delete its data.

A substitute the app suggested is an allergen itself.

Substitutes are screened against the allergens you have recorded for that child, so nothing suggested contains one of them. A substitute can still be a common allergen in its own right — cashew cream cheese is dairy-free but is a tree nut — and the app labels it where that is the case. Always read the label on the product you actually buy.

Are there accounts, subscriptions or ads?

None. SwapSafe is free, has no accounts to create, no subscriptions, no adverts and no tracking.

Not medical advice

SwapSafe offers general guidance and record-keeping only. It is not a medical device and not a substitute for professional advice. Always consult your GP or allergist, and in an emergency follow your action plan and call your local emergency services.
